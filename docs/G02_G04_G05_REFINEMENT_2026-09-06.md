## Self-critique and remaining limits

The former tablet/mobile hero overlay has become a more editorial image/text composition; desktop has less of a full-bleed campaign effect. This is an intentional tradeoff for a stable package safe area. On a 390 px-tall landscape home viewport, the image follows the copy below the fold; the jar is intact when reached. PDP uses a different short-landscape layout so its next action is immediately available.

Wide Gonseen packaging cannot be made as tall as a bottle without clipping or distortion. Its width, readable label and surrounding space now define its intended optical weight. Some source packshots remain softer than others; scaling does not restore missing source detail.

Generic template characteristics that remain are repeated editorial summaries, content gaps/repetition and the Halo triad. Those are G10/G11/G12; this layout task does not approve missing translations or claims. G08 editorial cover crops also remains open. The remaining audit work is therefore four findings, not release approval.
