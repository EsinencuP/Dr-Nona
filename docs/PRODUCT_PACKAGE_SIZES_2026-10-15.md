# Package sizes for every catalogue product

The owner extended the initial millilitre-only request: dry products must also show their package quantity, with no empty product-card metadata slots. Capsule counts may use the abbreviation `caps`.

The importer now preserves the units on the current Moldova category cards: millilitres, grams, capsules, tea bags and piece counts. All 50 published products have verified package sizes: 28 ml, 8 g, 10 capsule counts, two piece counts, one tea-bag count and one sample count. No weights are converted into volumes or serving counts. Current drinks cards specify 250 g for Slimseen Coffee Mix and Chocoseen, 300 g for Soupseen, and 24 tea bags for Gonseen Tea; these source units are retained rather than inferring portions from preparation instructions.

Sources rechecked on 2026-10-15: [creams](https://www.drnona.md/creams), [hygiene](https://www.drnona.md/hygiene), [perfumes](https://www.drnona.md/parfumes), [supplements](https://www.drnona.md/supplements), [drinks](https://www.drnona.md/drinks) and the [Top Samples Kit contents](https://www.drnona.md/top_samples_kit). The kit page lists eight individual samples, so its card displays 8 samples rather than a sum of different product volumes. Examples: OKSEEN 60 caps, RAVSEEN 100 caps, DND 60 pieces, Compressed Wipes 15 pieces, Mineral Lipstick 4 g and each bath salt 300 g.

The existing internal `product-volumes.json` snapshot now records a structured `packageSize` (positive quantity and a controlled unit) plus exact source text, matched product name/link and provenance. The importer matches names within their source text bounds, including centered multi-line salt names, and rejects missing or ambiguous matches before writing. Runtime generation rejects missing, stale or invalid measures for published products. Only the structured size reaches public projections; provenance links remain internal.

Card labels use RU `мл`, `г`, `caps`, `шт.`, `пак.`, `проб.` and RO `ml`, `g`, `caps`, `buc.`, `plicuri`, `mostre`. The existing position opposite the category and shared card layout remain. UI tooltips now describe package quantity rather than liquid volume alone.

Graph query/affected ownership: `Product` in `src/data.ts`, `ProductCard` in `src/components/ui.tsx`, the runtime generator and importer. Shared catalogue/recommendation cards, localized public datasets and bounded product-detail projections receive the change. There is no new persistence, external runtime API, CRM dependency, pricing or assortment change. Product copy, claims and release approval are unaffected.

The four RU/RO desktop/mobile package-size browser cases passed, including a final run with all cards rendered for geometry measurements. They assert all 50 labels, representative liquid/dry quantities and all-card geometry at 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px.
