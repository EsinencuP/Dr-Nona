# Reference Design Contract

## Keep

- Warm, tactile, premium editorial photography.
- Product integrated into a believable physical setting.
- Clear readable front label.
- Natural depth, soft directional light, and a visible support surface.
- Ingredient/category cues placed around the product, not across it.

## Change

- Reduce the product scale relative to the previous generated hero.
- Replace scattered decorative clutter with a maximum of five prop groups.
- Remove unrelated crystals and redundant bowls from Chocoseen.
- Use a calm site-palette clean card instead of a transparent final packshot.
- Give Lord a deep navy clean-card background with restrained gold contrast.

## Do not copy

- Exact reference layout or crop.
- Generated packaging, labels, logos, typography, or claims.
- Split-panel collage structure.
- Decorative objects that do not support the product category.

## Acceptance

- The product rests on the intended plane and does not float.
- No prop crosses the label or competes with the product.
- Product width is at most 56% in hero and 76% in clean card.
- Original packaging pixels remain unchanged except proportional resize and
  conservative post-resize sharpening.
