# Dr. Nona Noble Product Cards

## Direction

Create a two-image product-card system:

1. A clean ecommerce card on the Dr. Nona site palette.
2. A rich but restrained environmental hero where the real product is visibly
   supported by the scene rather than pasted over it.

## Visual hierarchy

- Product label is the primary focal point.
- Product occupies about 40-50% of hero height and no more than 56% of width.
- A visible support plane meets the bottom of the product around 74% of canvas
  height.
- Use three to five coherent prop groups, confined to the outer perimeter.
- Preserve negative space around the product silhouette.

## Palette

- Default clean card: `#F7FBFC`.
- Lord clean card: `#071827`, supported by white, navy, and restrained gold.
- Chocoseen hero: warm ivory, sand, travertine, cocoa brown, muted bronze.

## Fidelity

The source RGBA product is an immutable layer. Generate the environment only.
Do not redraw labels, logos, packaging geometry, colors, text, or proportions.
