# Dr. Nona Three-SKU Test Direction

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Three distinct premium product worlds unified by tactile natural materials,
realistic directional light, layered depth, and the immutable real product.
Night Cream is quiet nocturnal botanical care; Lord is cool coastal navy/gold;
Chocoseen is a warm cocoa morning ritual.

## 2. Color

Night Cream uses mineral white, muted moonlit sea-blue, olive, and restrained
gold. Lord uses `#071827`, `#0D2334`, pearl white, cool water blue, and
`#D0B274`. Chocoseen uses cream, cocoa brown, pale stone, and small Mineral
Light blue accents.

## 3. Typography

Generate no typography. All visible type, logos, and legal information belong
only to the protected source product.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

Square 2048 masters. Reserve 45-65% image height for the product and keep all
props outside its label/silhouette zone. Use a distinct spatial fingerprint for
each SKU.

## 5. Layout & Composition

Night Cream: low oval stone, moonlit arch, asymmetrical foliage. Lord: reflective
water plane, dark mineral monolith, wide coastal horizon. Chocoseen: warm stone
platform, cup, linen, cocoa ingredients arranged as a diagonal ritual.

## 6. Components

Each SKU has exactly two default assets: transparent clean cutout and one rich
hero. Optional detail slots are disabled.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

Still images only. Preserve enough clear perimeter for responsive product-card
crops and subtle site parallax if added later.

## 8. Voice & Brand

Calm, premium, mineral, evidence-led, and mature. Gold is a signature material,
not a dominant brown filter. Category props communicate context without claims.

## 9. Anti-patterns

No repeated beige arch template, duplicated package, generated labels, fake
ingredients, floating product, dense foliage, fantasy glow, excessive gold,
split-panel collage, people, hands, or text.
