# Dr. Nona Rich Product Scenes

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Premium mineral wellness photography with real tactile depth. The references set the
required richness: layered foreground, product platform, architectural or ritual
midground, natural detail, and believable directional light. Avoid generic empty beige
studios and repeated templates.

## 2. Color

Use the website's Mineral Light system: mineral white `#F7FBFC`, pale sea tones
`#E1F1F4` and `#C9E5EA`, restrained brand gold `#B99A5A`, and botanical green
`#6F927D`. Warm ivory may appear in materials, but it must not replace the cool
mineral identity. Lord scenes may use the separate navy/gold system.

## 3. Typography

Generate no typography. All package typography belongs to the immutable source PNG.
Reject generated letters, labels, marks, claims, or watermarks in the environment.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

Create one square scene per product. Reserve a clear product placement zone occupying
roughly 45-65% of image height. Arrange four to eight coherent material/prop groups
around—not across—the product silhouette.

## 5. Layout & Composition

Use asymmetry, foreground/midground/background depth, a credible support plane, and
category-specific composition. Each SKU receives a distinct spatial fingerprint; do
not reuse the same arch, podium, lighting direction, and prop arrangement.

## 6. Imagery & Art Direction

The product is an immutable protected layer. AI generates only an empty background
plate. Composite the original RGBA product locally. Cream: travertine, mineral arch,
olive and white blossom accents. Perfume: water reflection, carved mineral slab,
coastal light, restrained precious-material accent. Tea: morning ritual, cup, linen,
and verified botanicals.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

Not applicable to still exports. Preserve enough negative space for optional website
micro-motion or parallax without requiring animation in the master asset.

## 8. Responsive Behavior

The protected product and its critical label must remain within the central safe zone
so a square image can be cropped to common card ratios without losing packaging.

## 9. Accessibility

Keep the product legible against the environment, avoid low-contrast haze over the
label zone, excessive glare, strobing patterns, or detail that competes with the main
object.
