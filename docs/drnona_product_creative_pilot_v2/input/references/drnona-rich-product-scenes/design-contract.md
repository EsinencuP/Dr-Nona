# Design contract

## Evidence

- Provided Dynamic Cream reference: warm travertine, botanical and mineral detail,
  multi-angle editorial richness.
- Provided Chocoseen reference: tactile ingredient narrative, morning ritual,
  foreground-to-background depth.
- Provided perfume reference: mineral architecture, water, pale stone, gold accents,
  and campaign-scale polish.
- Dr. Nona project `COLOR_SYSTEM.md`: Mineral Light is cool, white/sea-led; gold and
  botanical green are rare supporting cues.

## Keep

- High material realism, natural directional light, visual storytelling, layered
  still-life depth, a premium support plane, and category-specific details.

## Adapt

- Translate warm beige references into Mineral Light.
- Produce one strong hero/lifestyle image rather than a four-panel collage.
- Use only ingredients confirmed by product metadata.
- Keep all props outside the protected product and label zone.

## Do not copy

- Product packaging or labels visible in references.
- Repeated beige arches and identical circular podiums for every SKU.
- Invented ingredients, claims, decorative text, or generated packages.
- Generic AI glow, floating objects, excessive gold, clutter, or split panels.

## Final stance

Every product-card set contains two essentials: a transparent catalog master and one
rich, category-specific environmental scene. Extra ingredient/detail assets are
explicit opt-ins, not defaults.
