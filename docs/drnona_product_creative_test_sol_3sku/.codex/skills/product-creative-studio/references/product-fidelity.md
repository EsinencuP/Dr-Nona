# Product Fidelity

The source product is an immutable layer. Preserve logo, label text, SKU,
geometry, cap, pump, colors, proportions, graphic elements, volume, trademarks,
and legal information.

Preferred method:

1. Keep the original RGBA pixels.
2. Generate an empty background plate.
3. Resize proportionally and composite locally.
4. Build shadow from the alpha mask.
5. Add reflection only on polished stone, water, or glass.
6. Limit color integration to edges, shadow, and local ambient light.
7. Compare source and placed product before approval.

Never invent an unseen rear label, open container, contents, new angle, separate
cap, pump, or box.
