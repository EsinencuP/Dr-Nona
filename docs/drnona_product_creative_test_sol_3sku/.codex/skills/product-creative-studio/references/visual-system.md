# Visual System

Use modern premium product photography, clean editorial ecommerce styling,
light backgrounds, natural materials, restrained architecture, soft studio or
daylight illumination, realistic contact, subtle depth, and two to five prop
groups.

Asset roles:

- clean-catalog: square, centered, 70-82% fill, no decor.
- hero: square 2048 master, product 45-65% height, empty central zone.
- ingredients: only confirmed ingredients; otherwise abstract materials.
- lifestyle/detail: plausible atmosphere without inventing a new product angle.

Avoid neon, dark or crowded scenes, fantasy, oversized flowers, duplicate
products, people without permission, text, watermarks, and generic AI styling.
